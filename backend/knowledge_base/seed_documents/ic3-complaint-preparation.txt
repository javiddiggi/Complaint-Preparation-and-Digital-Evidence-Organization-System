Cyber Assist summary of official guidance — United States jurisdiction
Source: FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3), Frequently Asked Questions
Official source: https://www.ic3.gov/Home/FAQ
This is a short Cyber Assist summary, not the official source text or legal advice. Check the current IC3 instructions and the reporting authority applicable to your location.

The IC3 says complaint accuracy and completeness matter. Its FAQ identifies information that may be requested, including complainant contact details; financial account, transaction date, amount and recipient details when relevant; subject contact or website details when known; specific incident details; and email headers if available or applicable. These are examples of information to report when known and relevant, not permission to guess or fill in missing values.

The IC3 states that it does not collect evidence attachments. Keep original supporting records in a secure location. Its FAQ lists examples such as payment receipts, wire or cryptocurrency receipts, electronic copies of email with full headers, web pages, phone bills, chat transcripts, telephony logs, and technical logs where applicable. Retain the records in case an investigating agency requests them.
