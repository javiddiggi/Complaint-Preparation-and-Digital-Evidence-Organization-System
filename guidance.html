01

Report online financial fraud

Keep transaction identifiers, dates, amounts, payment service details, and related communications available.

Record the transaction date and time if known, amount, UTR or transaction ID, payment channel, recipient details available to you, and the relevant bank or payment-app reference. Do not share passwords, PINs, or OTPs.
02

Preserve digital evidence

Keep original files, relevant message context, URLs, and available timestamps.

Keep originals unchanged. Record where each item came from and when it was collected, if known. Avoid cropping or editing the only copy of a screenshot. Preserve surrounding messages when relevant.
03

Prepare a clear incident summary

Describe what happened in chronological order and distinguish known facts from assumptions.

Include how contact began, what was requested, what action occurred, what loss or impact followed, and what records support each detail. Mark uncertain dates or amounts for review.
04

Review before submission

Check names, dates, amounts, source records, and the final wording.

Verify the draft against original records, remove any inaccurate statement, attach relevant supporting material, and use the official reporting channel appropriate to your location and incident.
