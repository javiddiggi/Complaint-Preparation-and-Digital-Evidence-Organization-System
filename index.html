PRIVACY & LIMITATIONS

Review every fact before taking action.

AI-generated information may be inaccurate. Verify all facts and review the complaint before submission. Cyber Assist prepares drafts but does not submit complaints to authorities. Sign up and login are demonstration-only and are not secure production authentication. External AI services are optional and require your explicit consent.
